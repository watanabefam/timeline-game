        How to play
        
        
          
          
        1. Pick a deck, then (optionally) filter it by age / week / continent.
          2. 
          
        2. Read the mystery event. Its year stays hidden.
          3. 
          
        3. Choose a gap — before, between, or after the placed events.
          4. 
          
        4. Every card you place is worth 3 points; each slip on it costs 1 (never below 0). Perfect runs earn 3 × the cards placed.
          5. 
          
        5. Wrong placement bounces you back to try again — slips reduce that card's points to zero, so clean runs earn the most.
          6. 
        
        
        
        Same-year rule: if two events share a year, either side counts. Every run is a fresh random puzzle from your chosen events, and wrong answers never end the run — they just cost points.
